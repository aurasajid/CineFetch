👋 Hello, CineFetch!

Your ultimate movie streaming companion — learn how to get started below.

Step 1

Search for any movie

Enter the IMDb ID (e.g., tt0468569) in the search bar. You can also search by movie title if you have the exact ID. CineFetch will load the movie instantly.

Step 2

Select season & episode

For TV series, specify the season and episode numbers. CineFetch will load the correct episode from the embed source. Leave blank for movies.

Step 3

Sign in & sync history

Create an account or sign in with Google to save your watch history. Your progress syncs across devices, so you can resume anywhere.
